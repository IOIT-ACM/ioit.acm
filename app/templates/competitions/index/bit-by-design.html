Objectives

Bit by Design is a focused, time-bound web design competition centered on clarity, creativity, and design reasoning. Participants work individually on a shared design prompt using a mandatory Figma template, creating original web interfaces under strict constraints. The competition is designed to evaluate participants' ability to communicate ideas visually and reason through design decisions under pressure.

Rules

  • Individual participation only — no teams.
  • All designs must be created using the provided mandatory Figma template.
  • Participants interpret a shared design prompt and challenge brief.
  • Customization is allowed for colors, typography, and visual styling within the template constraints.
  • All designs must be created live during the event duration — no pre-made work.
  • Submissions are evaluated through an anonymous, structured peer-evaluation and voting system.
  • Evaluation criteria:
    • Clarity: Is the design easy to understand?
    • Hierarchy: Is information structured logically?
    • Originality: Does it show creative thinking?
    • Design Reasoning: Are design choices intentional and well-justified?
  • The event is hosted on a custom-built platform for submissions and voting.
  • If you encounter any issues, refresh the page or contact the organizers.
  • Enjoy the competition and give it your best!
